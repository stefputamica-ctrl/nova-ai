Questions

Is Nova free?

Yes. Nova is free on Android, Windows, iPhone and the web, with no ads. It uses your own free Google Gemini key, which you get at aistudio.google.com in about a minute.

Does Nova speak Romanian?

Yes. Talk or type in Romanian or English and Nova answers in the same language, with a natural voice for that language.

Can I talk to Nova without typing?

Yes. Tap the microphone to say one thing, or switch on conversation mode to talk back and forth hands-free. Nova answers out loud and then listens again.

Can Nova set reminders?

Yes. Say “remind me at 6 to practise guitar”. On Android the reminder pops up even when Nova is closed.

Is my data private?

Your Gemini key stays on your device. Chats stay on your device unless you log in and turn on sync. There are no ads and no tracking.

How do I install Nova on iPhone?

Open the Nova web app in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It opens full screen like a normal app.
